Hazel Chess

Draw arrows on your phone — just like chess.com desktop.

How to draw arrows
  • Tap 🖊️ Draw, then drag from one square to another for an arrow.
  • Tap a square to highlight it. Tap again to remove.
  • Pick a color: green, red, blue, or orange.
  • Or long-press a square (then drag for an arrow) — no need to turn Draw on.
  • Two-finger drag also draws an arrow.
  • On a computer: right-click drag, just like chess.com. Shift = red, Alt = blue, Ctrl = orange.
  • Arrows are private — your opponent never sees them.
